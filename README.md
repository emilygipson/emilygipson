## Emily Gipson

Biological oceanographer working on plankton population genomics and isotope biogeochemistry. Newly minted PhD.

Low-coverage whole-genome resequencing pipelines and genotype-likelihood based analyses, de novo transcriptome assembly, Bayesian inference and isotope mixing models, forward population genetic simulation in SLiM.

Interested in pop gen signatures of weird life cycles and boom-bust population dynamics.
