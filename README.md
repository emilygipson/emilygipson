## Emily Gipson

Biological oceanographer working on plankton population genomics and biogeochemistry. PhD candidate at the University of Georgia (Skidaway Institute of Oceanography), defending June 2026.

My dissertation focuses on bloom-forming pelagic tunicates (*Dolioletta gegenbauri* and salps) in the South Atlantic Bight, integrating low-coverage whole-genome sequencing, forward population genetic simulation, and stable isotope biogeochemistry. Interested in pop gen signatures of weird life cycles and boom-bust population dynamics.

low-coverage whole-genome resequencing pipelines and genotype-likelihood-based analyses, de novo transcriptome assembly, Bayesian inference and isotope mixing models, forward population genetic simulation
